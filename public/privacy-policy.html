Cyberstition ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy through a Privacy-First Architecture that ensures all analysis happens locally on your device. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices when you use the Cyberstition scam detection application.

The Bottom Line: We don't collect, store, or transmit your personal data or the content you analyze. Everything stays on your device.

1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 What is Cyberstition?

Cyberstition is a privacy-first scam detection tool that helps you identify:

  • Phishing and Fraud: Suspicious messages, emails, and social media contacts
  • Fake Profiles: Impersonation and deceptive social media accounts
  • Email Spoofing: Forged email headers and routing anomalies
  • Image Manipulation: Hidden metadata and manipulation indicators
  • Deepfakes: AI-generated content and synthetic media

1.2 Our Privacy Commitment

Cyberstition is built on a fundamental principle: your data belongs to you. All analysis runs entirely within your browser using client-side processing. We have no servers that store your content, no databases tracking your investigations, and no analytics monitoring your behavior.

2. PRIVACY-FIRST ARCHITECTURE

2.1 Client-Side Processing

How Cyberstition Works:

All Processing Happens in Your Browser:

  • Message Analysis: Text processing, pattern detection, and AI detection algorithms run client-side
  • Profile Verification: Social profile analysis performed entirely in your browser
  • Email Analysis: Email header parsing and routing verification done locally
  • Image Inspection: Metadata extraction and manipulation detection run on-device
  • Pattern Matching: Pre-loaded threat pattern library matches locally without server calls
  • Risk Scoring: All confidence calculations and risk assessments computed client-side

Your content is never transmitted to our servers or any third-party service during analysis.

2.2 Offline-Capable

Cyberstition works completely offline once loaded. You can disconnect from the internet and continue using all analysis features. This proves that no data is being transmitted during your investigation sessions.

2.3 No Account Required

Cyberstition does not require account creation, login, or authentication to use core features. You can purchase report credits using a license key system that requires no personal information.

3. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT

Cyberstition explicitly does NOT collect, access, store, or transmit:

Content You Analyze:

  • Messages, emails, or text content submitted for analysis
  • Social media profiles or usernames you investigate
  • Email headers or routing information
  • Images, photos, or files you upload
  • Investigation results, risk scores, or findings
  • Scan session details or evidence

Personal Information:

  • Name, email address, or contact information
  • IP addresses or location data
  • Device identifiers or fingerprints
  • Browser information or user agent strings
  • Browsing history or navigation patterns

Behavioral Data:

  • App usage analytics or feature tracking
  • Click streams or interaction patterns
  • Session duration or frequency
  • Error reports or crash logs

Due to our client-side architecture, this data is processed entirely in your browser. It never leaves your device.

4. LOCAL STORAGE

4.1 What We Store Locally

Cyberstition stores the following data locally in your browser using localStorage and IndexedDB. This data never leaves your device:

Local Storage Contents:

  • Scan Sessions: Your investigation history, evidence, findings, and risk assessments
  • Report Credits: Your purchased credit balance and redeemed license keys
  • App Preferences: Theme selection, analysis settings, and UI preferences
  • Usage Counters: Monthly scan count for free tier limits (stored locally only)
  • Onboarding Status: Whether you've completed the initial tour

Important: This data is stored only on your device. It is not synced to cloud services or transmitted to Cyberstition servers.

4.2 Clearing Your Data

You have complete control over your local data:

  • Clear Browser Data: Use your browser's "Clear Site Data" or "Clear Cookies and Site Data" to permanently delete all Cyberstition data
  • Delete Individual Scans: Remove specific scan sessions from your dashboard
  • No Recovery: Once deleted from your device, data cannot be recovered (we don't have copies)

4.3 Data Portability

All your scan data is stored in standard formats (JSON) in browser localStorage. You can export this data at any time using browser developer tools or a localStorage export extension.

5.1 Payment Processors

Credit pack purchases are processed by third-party payment providers:

Stripe, Inc.:

  • Industry-standard payment processing
  • Subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy
  • PCI DSS compliant

Gumroad:

  • Digital product payment platform
  • Subject to Gumroad's Privacy Policy

What Cyberstition Does NOT Receive:

  • Credit card numbers or payment details
  • Billing addresses or personal information
  • Purchase history or transaction details
  • Email addresses (handled by payment processor only)

5.2 License Key System

After purchase, you receive a license key via email from the payment processor. You redeem this key locally in the Cyberstition app. The redemption process:

  • Validates the key format client-side
  • Stores the redeemed key in localStorage
  • Credits are managed entirely locally
  • No server validation or tracking

6. COOKIES AND TRACKING

6.1 No Cookies

Cyberstition does not use cookies of any kind. We don't need them because:

  • No user authentication or sessions
  • No server-side tracking
  • All data stored in localStorage, not cookies

6.2 No Analytics or Tracking

Cyberstition does NOT use:

  • Google Analytics or similar analytics platforms
  • Tracking pixels or web beacons
  • Fingerprinting or device identification
  • Third-party scripts from advertising networks
  • Session replay or user recording tools
  • A/B testing or conversion tracking
  • Social media widgets or share buttons with tracking

We have no visibility into your app usage, behavior, or patterns. We cannot track which features you use, how often you use them, or what results you get.

6.3 No Advertising

Cyberstition is funded by credit pack purchases, not advertising. We display no ads and never engage in behavioral targeting or data monetization.

7. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

7.1 No External API Calls During Analysis

Cyberstition makes zero external API calls during scam analysis. All processing happens within your browser using pre-loaded algorithms and pattern libraries.

7.2 External Connections

The only external connections occur when:

1. Payment Links:

  • Clicking "Purchase Credits" opens Stripe or Gumroad in a new tab
  • You interact directly with their platforms
  • Subject to their privacy policies, not ours

2. Hosting Infrastructure:

  • Cyberstition is hosted on Vercel or similar CDN
  • Standard web hosting logs (IP address, timestamp, accessed files)
  • Logs retained for 7 days for security monitoring only
  • No personally identifiable information linked to logs

8. DATA SECURITY

8.1 Security by Design

Cyberstition's privacy-first architecture provides inherent security benefits:

Security Features:

  • No Server Storage: We can't lose data we never collect
  • No Data Breaches: There's no central database to breach
  • Client-Side Only: Data exists only on your device, under your control
  • HTTPS: All web traffic encrypted with TLS 1.3
  • Content Security Policy: Prevents XSS and injection attacks
  • Subresource Integrity: Ensures script integrity

8.2 Browser Security

Your data security depends on your device and browser security:

  • Browser LocalStorage: Protected by browser same-origin policy
  • Device Security: Keep your device password-protected and updated
  • Browser Updates: Use the latest browser version for security patches
  • Incognito/Private Mode: Data cleared when closing private browsing session

8.3 Vulnerabilities

If you discover a security vulnerability in Cyberstition, please report it responsibly:

Email: security@cyberstition.com

11. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Cyberstition is not directed at children under 13 years of age.
